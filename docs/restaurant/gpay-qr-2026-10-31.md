# GPay QR checkout preview — 31 October 2026

The owner requested QR implementation, then clarified that merchant details are not available and requested a mockup. Checkout now offers **Show GPay QR** when GPay or split payment is selected. The preview uses the current theme and displays the exact GPay amount, restaurant/payee name and payment context. Cash-only checkout hides the button. Full GPay requests the whole bill; split payment uses only the GPay share, independently of cash tender/change.

With no verified merchant setup, the preview explicitly says **DEMO · NO PAYMENT** and explains that it cannot receive money. Its QR encodes JSON identified as `vgl-pos-qr-demo`, with `payment:false`, exact amount and a local reference. It contains no UPI payment URI or sample bank destination. Viewing it does not populate merchant settings, save a sale, consume a bill number or confirm a payment. The normal manual checkout remains available after returning to payment.

Once the owner supplies and verifies their actual merchant UPI ID/payee through the existing Settings form, the same view can generate a local `upi://pay` QR using pa/pn/am/cu and the current operation reference. This is a request for payment; the cashier must confirm receipt independently. No payment gateway, network settlement check, bank callback, cloud account or UPI intent launch was added. Real merchant scanning/settlement remains unverified. Existing receipt QR behavior and historical sale snapshots were not changed.

## Implementation

- `restaurant/upi-qr.js`: exact-paise amount selection, verified merchant URI validation/encoding, non-payable demo payload and lazy local QR encoder. Existing qrcode 1.5.4 dependency is reused; no runtime dependency added. Four-module quiet zone, black on white, 420px PNG.
- `restaurant/payment-qr-ui.js`: display-only dialog, localized text, generation cancellation and stale-amount invalidation. Closing clears the image; delayed work cannot put an old QR back. Back restores focus to the QR button and keeps the entered payment amounts.
- `restaurant/labels.js`: centralized English/Tamil QR copy.
- Original `pos.html`, `payment-view.js`, `pos.js` and `restaurant/style.css`: small checkout integration plus a responsive QR dialog. Billing menu/cart arrangement retained.
- `tests/restaurant/upi-qr.test.js`: exact amount/URI encoding, input validation, split cash/change independence, non-payable demo and unchanged settings.
- `tests/restaurant-browser/payment-qr.spec.js`: demo/full/split flow, no sale/config writes, late edit clearing, invalid share hiding, focus return, verified disposable merchant fixture and compact landscape bounds.
- `android/app/build.gradle`: version 1.0.9 (10), so the Android update can be distinguished from 1.0.8.

No schema change, tax change, ledger reset, replacement product assets or shared-backup deletion is involved.
